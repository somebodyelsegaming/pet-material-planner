Reading a row: every percentage is a share of the whole climb to max level for one pet, not of anything owned. "6 of 10 at 23%" means six milestones unlocked for under a quarter of what that pet will ever eat. The invested figure averages the four material shares so none dominates; the columns beside it show how sharply they diverge — Medallions appear only from the Advance to 50.

Caveat: this counts Advance milestones, not raw stats, which the cost tables do not carry. If late Advances grant outsized stats, the real curve is flatter than shown.
